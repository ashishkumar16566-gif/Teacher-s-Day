To the person whose lessons stayed

With sincere gratitude,

There are people we call teachers because they teach us from a syllabus. And then there are people who teach us simply through the way they speak, guide, encourage and carry themselves.

You are one of those people.

Perhaps you never set out to become a memorable part of someone's journey. Yet somewhere along the way, a piece of your wisdom found its place in my mind—a thought, a piece of advice, an example, or perhaps just the confidence you gave when it was needed.

“The most meaningful lessons are not always the ones written on a board. Some are the ones that quietly change the way we see ourselves.”

So on this Teacher's Day, I don't want to thank you merely for what you have taught. I want to thank you for the influence behind those lessons—for making learning feel meaningful, for encouraging growth, and for reminding people that they are capable of more than they sometimes believe.

Years may pass, classrooms may change, and pages may be forgotten. But the right words, given at the right moment, have a way of travelling much farther than we imagine.

May you always know that the knowledge you share and the encouragement you give do not end when a conversation or a lesson ends. They continue in the people who carry them forward.

Thank you for being a lesson worth remembering. ❤️

With warmth, respect & heartfelt gratitude

🕯️

A Light

For making difficult things feel possible when the path was unclear.

For leaving behind something that continues to grow long after the lesson.
